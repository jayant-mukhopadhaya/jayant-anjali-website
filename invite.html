Mapusa in the Garden. Saturday 21 November 2026, 3:30 PM to 6:00 PM. Enjoy a vibrant and whimsical start to the weekend with a slice of Mapusa Market, an essential Goan experience. Dress Code

Formal evening wear in deep, jewel tones.

Aiburo Bhaat. Saturday 21 November 2026, 7:30 PM onwards. “Aiburo” translates to bachelor, and “bhaat” means rice in Bengali. It refers to a pre-wedding celebration, highlighting the importance of food (especially for Jayant) in this ritual. The purpose of this ritual is to give the bride and groom a final feast before the wedding.
