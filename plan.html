Which Airport?

Goa has two airports. Mopa (GOX) is the newer terminal in North Goa, closer to the venue. Dabolim (GOI) is the older, more established airport in South Goa. We recommend flying into GOX if your airline serves it. GOI is perfectly fine too; it's about 45–60 min from the venue.

Entry Requirements

Visas

India offers an e-Visa for citizens of most countries. Apply well in advance: processing typically takes 3–5 business days, but allow at least 2 weeks for peace of mind.

e-Visa (Tourist)

Available to 160+ countries. Apply at indianvisaonline.gov.in. The 30-day and 1-year e-Tourist visas both allow double/multiple entry.

On Arrival

Goa's Dabolim Airport is a designated e-Visa entry port. Carry your approval letter and a passport photo, and immigration will issue the stamp on arrival.

Need Help?

If your country isn't e-Visa eligible, reach out to us directly. Always verify requirements with your country's Indian consulate for the most current information.
