Flights & Arrival

The first event kicks off at 3:00 PM on Saturday, November 21st. We suggest that you arrive the day before, on the 20th evening, to give yourself time to settle in before our first event. If you can't come in the day before, we recommend landing no later than 12:00 PM. Most domestic hubs have morning flights that easily make that window.

We'd love for you to make a holiday of it. Come for a week! Goa in November is absolutely gorgeous and we'll have plenty of recommendations for things to do.

For

International Guests

To

Which Airport?

Goa has two airports. Mopa (GOX) is the newer terminal in North Goa, closer to the venue. Dabolim (GOI) is the older, more established airport in South Goa. We recommend flying into GOX.

Independent

Other Options Nearby

Candolim, Calangute, Baga, and Vagator are all 10–20 min from the venue and offer a wide range of hotels, villas, and guest houses. Visas

India offers an e-Visa to citizens of 160+ countries. Apply at indianvisaonline.gov.in for the most current requirements based on your nationality. Processing typically takes 3–5 business days, but we'd recommend applying at least 2 months in advance to be safe.

Getting Around

There's no Uber, Lyft, or Ola in Goa, but getting around is straightforward with a little planning. We will be organizing transport between the hotel and the wedding venue so you do not have to worry about that. Our hotel choice is even walking distance from our venue.

For your other explorations we can help coordinate the options below.

Self-drive

Rental Car or Scooter

A scooter is the ideal mode of transport in Goa, particularly if your planned explorations are within a 30-45 minute radius of your home base. For longer explorations, particularly inland, we'd recommend a rental car for a little more comfort. The rental agencies typically work over Whatsapp so reach out and we can share contact details of rental agencies.

Driven

Hire a Driver

If you'd rather have a more plush experience, or are not comfortable driving on the right side of the road, we can share contacts for reliable local drivers who can be hired by the day or half-day.

Extended Stay

We'd love for you to make a holiday of it. Goa in November is lovely. It is just after the monsoons so it is verdant. Goa covers a very large area and you can choose your adventure based on what kind of holiday you want to have.

Activity Focused

North Goa

If you want to surf, lounge at a beach shack, be close to great food, and be surrounded by people, North Goa is your place. This is where we have spent the most time in Goa and we have a whole lot of recommendations.

Quiet & Relaxed

South Goa

If you want a more zen experience, South Goa is your place. Long stretches of empty beach, yoga retreats, and lush countryside. This is likely where we will end up after the wedding to relax and unwind. The stays in this part are more luxurious and the areas are more remote but still accessible by car.
