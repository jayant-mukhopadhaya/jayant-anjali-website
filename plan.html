For

Route through Mumbai (BOM), Delhi (DEL), Bengaluru (BLR), or Chennai (MAA) for the best connections. We'd recommend avoiding Middle East stopover hubs given the current situation. Arriving on Friday the 20th is the safest bet; you'll skip the rush and have time to explore.

To

Which Airport?

Goa has two airports. Mopa (GOX) is the newer terminal in North Goa, closer to the venue. Dabolim (GOI) is the older, more established airport in South Goa. We recommend flying into GOX if your airline serves it. GOI is perfectly fine too; it's about 45–60 min from the venue.

Visas

India offers an e-Visa to citizens of 160+ countries. Apply at indianvisaonline.gov.in for the most current requirements based on your nationality. Processing typically takes 3–5 business days, but we'd recommend applying at least 2 months in advance to be safe.

Getting Around

There's no Uber or Ola in Goa, but getting around is straightforward with a little planning. We will be organizing transport to and from the wedding venue so you do not have to worry about that. Our hotel choice is even walking distance from our venue.

For your other explorations we can help coordinate the options below.

Rental Car or Scooter

A scooter is the quitessential mode of transport in Goa, particularly if you're planned explorations are within a 30-45 minute radius of your home base. For longer explorations, particularly inland, we'd recommend a rental car for a little more comfort. The rental agencies typically work over Whatsapp so reach out and we can share contact details of rental agencies.

Hire a Driver

If you'd rather have a more plush experience, or are not comfortable driving on the right side of the road, we can share contacts for reliable local drivers who can be hired by the day or half-day.

Extended Stay

We'd love for you to make a holiday of it. Goa in November is lovely. It is just after the monsoons so it is verdant. Goa covers a very large area and you can choose your adventure based on what kind of holiday you want to have.

Activity Focused · North Goa

If you want to surf, lounge at a beach shack, be close to great food,and be surrounded by people, North Goa is your place. This is where we have spent the most time in Goa and we have a whole lot of recommendations.

Quiet & Relaxed · South Goa

If you want a more zen experience, South Goa is your place. Long stretches of empty beach, yoga retreats, and lush countryside. This is likely where we will end up after the wedding to relax and unwind. The stays in this part are more luxurious, the areas are more remote but still accessible by car.
