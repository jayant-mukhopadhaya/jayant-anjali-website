FAQs

Answers to common questions

How far in advance should I book travel?

Now. November is peak season in Goa and flights fill up fast. Book flights as soon as you know you're coming, ideally by August. You can book your hotel rooms now. International guests should also start their e-Visa process at least 2–3 months before travel. See the Plan Your Trip page for full guidance, including information for extended stays.

When should I arrive?

The first event starts at 3:00 PM on Saturday, November 21st. We'd recommend arriving the day before, Friday the 20th, so you have a chance to relax before the wedding festivities begin. If arriving on the 21st, aim for no later than 12:00 PM. Check the Plan Your Trip page for airport and flight guidance.

Will there be transport to the venue?

We're arranging transport between the hotel block and the venue so you don't have to worry about that transport. More details will be shared closer to the date.

What will the weather be like?

November in Goa is lovely. The monsoon has fully passed and you can expect warm, dry days around 28–32°C (82–90°F) with cool evenings. Light, breathable fabrics are ideal. Sunscreen is a must if you're spending any time outdoors. Bring a hat, sunglasses and anything else you may need to keep cool!

What's the dress code?

We're working on outfit recommendations and will share more specific guidance on dress codes and colour palettes soon.

Are children invited?

We would love for you to relax and enjoy your time with us, fully. So we kindly ask that guests make childcare arrangements in advance. The one exception: nursing infants are of course always welcome. Please don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need help planning around this.

What are the food and dietary options?

We are so excited to share the food that we grew up eating, with everyone. There will be vegetarian options at every meal. If you have specific dietary requirements (vegan, gluten-free, severe allergies), please note them on your RSVP and we'll make sure you're taken care of.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We'll have a professional photographer and videographer capturing everything. During the ceremony we ask that guests are fully present and refrain from using phones. We'll have plenty of professional shots to share. For all other times and events, we encourage everyone to take photos and videos and share them with us.

I have another question not listed here.
