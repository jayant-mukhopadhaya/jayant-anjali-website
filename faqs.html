FAQs

Answers to common questions

How far in advance should I book travel?

Now. November is peak season in Goa and flights fill up fast. Book flights as soon as you know you're coming — ideally by August. We're finalising a hotel block near the venue and will share details soon, but if you want flexibility, start researching accommodation in North Goa in the meantime. International guests should also start their e-Visa process at least 2–3 weeks before travel. See the Travel & Stay page for full guidance.

When should I arrive?

The first event starts at 2:00 PM on Saturday, November 21st. We'd recommend landing by late morning at the latest — aim for no later than 10:00 AM. If you're flying in from abroad, arriving the evening of Friday the 20th is a much safer and more relaxed option. Check the Travel & Stay page for airport and flight guidance.

Will there be transport to the venue?

We're arranging transport between the hotel block (details coming soon) and the venue. More details will be shared closer to the date. If you're staying independently, we can help with trusted local cab recommendations — just reach out.

What will the weather be like?

November in Goa is lovely — the monsoon has fully passed and you can expect warm, dry days around 28–32°C (82–90°F) with cool evenings. Light, breathable fabrics are ideal. Sunscreen is a must if you're spending any time outdoors. Bring a hat, sunglasses and anything else you may need to keep cool!

What's the dress code?

We'd love for guests to embrace the festive spirit — the events across the weekend will range from celebratory to celebratory, so come dressed for a party. More specific guidance on dress codes and colour palettes will be shared closer to the date.

Are children invited?

Our celebrations are adult-only, so we kindly ask that guests make childcare arrangements in advance. The one exception: nursing infants are of course always welcome — please don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need help planning around this.

What are the food and dietary options?

Indian weddings are legendary for their food and ours will be no exception. There will be vegetarian options at every meal. If you have specific dietary requirements — vegan, gluten-free, severe allergies — please note them on your RSVP and we'll make sure you're taken care of.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We'll have a professional photographer and videographer capturing everything. During the ceremony we ask that guests are fully present and refrain from using phones — we'll have plenty of professional shots to share. The Party Evening is fair game!

I have another question not listed here.

Please reach out to us directly at jayantandanjali@gmail.com and we'll be happy to help. You can also check the Travel & Stay page for logistics information.
