Travel & Stay

Everything you need to get to Goa

Getting There

Flights

The first event kicks off at 2:00 PM on Saturday, November 21st. To give yourself time to settle in, we recommend landing by late morning — aim for no later than 11:00 AM. Most domestic hubs have morning flights that easily make that window. If you're coming from abroad, we'd strongly suggest arriving the evening of Friday the 20th to start the weekend relaxed.

We'd love for you to make a holiday of it — come for a week! Goa in November is absolutely gorgeous and we'll have plenty of recommendations for things to do.

Which Airport?

Goa has two airports: Dabolim (GOI) — the older, more established airport in South Goa — and Mopa (GOX) — the newer terminal in North Goa, closer to the venue. We recommend flying into GOX if your airline serves it. GOI is perfectly fine too; it's about 45–60 min from the venue depending on where you're staying.

Morning Connections

From Mumbai (BOM): flights take ~1 hour — a 9:00 AM departure gets you there comfortably. From Delhi (DEL): ~2.5 hours — aim for a 7:00–8:00 AM departure. From Bengaluru (BLR) or Chennai (MAA): ~1–1.5 hours — morning options are plentiful. Airlines include IndiGo, Air India, Akasa, and SpiceJet.

International Guests

Route through Mumbai (BOM), Delhi (DEL), Bengaluru (BLR), or Chennai (MAA) for the best connections. We'd recommend avoiding Middle East stopover hubs given the current situation. Arriving on Friday the 20th is the safest bet — you'll skip the rush and have time to explore.

North Goa

Closest to the venue. Vibrant beaches, great restaurants, and a buzzing nightlife scene. Candolim, Calangute, Baga, and Vagator are all excellent bases — each with their own character. Most places are 10–20 min from the venue.

South Goa

If you're extending the trip, South Goa offers a completely different pace — quieter, lusher, with long stretches of largely empty beach. Palolem, Colva, and Benaulim are beautiful for a few extra days of unwinding. About 45–60 min from the venue.

Entry Requirements

Visas

India offers an e-Visa for citizens of most countries. Apply well in advance — processing typically takes 3–5 business days but allow at least 2 weeks for peace of mind.

e-Visa (Tourist)

Available to 160+ countries. Apply at indianvisaonline.gov.in. The 30-day and 1-year e-Tourist visas both allow double/multiple entry.

On Arrival

Goa's Dabolim Airport is a designated e-Visa entry port. Carry your approval letter and a passport photo — immigration issues the stamp on arrival.

Need Help?

If your country isn't e-Visa eligible, reach out to us directly. Always verify requirements with your country's Indian consulate for the most current information.
